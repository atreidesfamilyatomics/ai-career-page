## How to use this document

This document is the AI's working memory. When someone visits your career page and asks a question, Claude reads this file and responds as you — accurately, specifically, and on-brand.

**The most important sections:**
- **Section 7 (Pre-Answered FAQs)** — Write honest, complete answers to the hard questions. Claude will use these verbatim. Don't hedge.
- **Section 8 (Fit Rubric)** — Define your strong fits and poor fits explicitly. This is what makes the fit score honest. Most people skip it. Don't skip it.

**Format tips:**
- Be specific. "Generated 45 qualified leads on day one" is better than "drove pipeline results."
- Include the context. Recruiters need company size, stage, and your scope — not just what you did.
- Write how you actually talk, not how you think a LinkedIn profile should sound.
